## 1. The placement, verified

**What E9 measures in the serving literature's terms.** Entry 0023 registers the per-token deviation as
computed in content space: K with RoPE stripped, V unrotated, both compared "exactly as the pinned scorer
does" [0023, line 24–25 of the entry]. Position-independent caching (PIC) reuses an independently cached
chunk at a new position behind a new prefix; its free step is rotating cached keys to their new positions;
what remains is the cross-context residual that every PIC repair method (selective recompute, boundary
recompute, learned repair) exists to fix. E9-same's centered per-token δ *is* that residual, measured on the
receiver's own KV for the same token in the sender's prompt and in the receiver's. f*(τ) is the fraction an
oracle would recompute at tolerance τ, which is the PIC question's own quantity, read as a floor [0027].

**Why this fits better than "re-render or retrain."** The two-axis frame spends §1 introducing an unrun
weights-axis experiment (E-RL, designed and unregistered) and then measures only the context axis. The PIC
frame introduces the axis the paper measured, places it in a line with twenty published neighbours, and
frees ≈0.5 page. Under it, "re-render" stays as the mechanism verb for how a handoff arises (the receiver
rebuilds its prompt from the sender's content), defined once on first use, and "non-prefix reuse" is what a
serving reviewer recognises it as.

**What the pivot exposes, to be named in the body rather than discovered by a reviewer.**
1. *No downstream-quality number.* PIC papers report F1 or accuracy against full recompute; KVShareArena
   scores the fraction of the no-cache-to-full-recompute gap recovered. E9 reports KV deviation only, and 0029
   states that generation quality after reuse is not established. One scope sentence in §1, one in §6.
2. *The τ_K anchor.* In a PIC frame a reviewer asks why the same-model tolerance is a cross-model mapper's
   held-out shortfall. The τ ladder [0029, 0036] is the answer and should move from a descriptive aside in
   §4 into the body beside the first HOLDS.
3. *Floor, not method.* The abstract must say "floor" before it says "zero", so f* = 0 is never read as
   "reuse is lossless." Under 0027 it reads: no scheme needs to recompute more than zero at this tolerance;
   no scheme is shown to achieve it.

## 2. Draft §1 — Non-prefix reuse at a handoff (≈ 0.6 page) — *proposed prose*

Prefix caching reuses a KV cache only where the next prompt repeats the previous one byte for byte from the
start. A handoff between agents breaks that condition by construction: the receiver re-renders its prompt
from the sender's content, so the same tokens reappear at new positions behind a new prefix, with new tokens
at the seams. On the public record this is exactly what a handoff looks like. Across 2,904 public
trajectories we observe 68 mid-trajectory switches on 60 measurable trajectories; none of the 68 is
byte-identical to what the sender processed, at a median overlap of 0.988 between the receiver's prompt and
sender-processed content [0018, FROZEN]. The content relocates; it does not go missing.

The serving literature has a name for this. Position-independent caching reuses an independently cached
chunk at any position and repairs the cross-context loss by recomputing a subset of tokens (§2, related
work). Every method in that line is evaluated on compositions built for the benchmark: retrieved passages,
or reports written by other agents to a fixed template. None measures the loss at a real handoff inside a
long-horizon trajectory, and none, in the work we surveyed, at prompts of the length measured here
[VERIFY the length claim against §6 before writing it; if unverified, drop the clause]. That is the
measurement this paper makes.

The quantity. For each token the receiver's prompt shares with the sender's, we compare the receiver's own
key for that token at its position in the receiver's prompt with the receiver's key for the same token at its
position in the sender's prompt, in content space (rotary encoding removed; values carry none) [0023]. The
free step of every position-independent method, rotating a cached key to its new position, is therefore
already taken; what we measure is the residual that selective recompute exists to repair. One statistic
reads it: f*(τ), the fraction of matched tokens whose centered deviation exceeds a tolerance τ, which is the
fraction an oracle would have to recompute. The tolerance τ_K = 0.3186 is a k = 1 cross-model linear map's
own held-out shortfall on its calibration text (1 − R²) [0016/0020]; a token needs recompute if the same
model at a new position disagrees with itself by more than that map disagrees with the target it was fit
to. HOLDS is f* ≤ 0.15, DEGRADES f* ≥ 0.50 [0023; CHECK the CacheBlend attribution of 0.15 — v2 cites it as
"CacheBlend's achieved budget" and the program's own review notes the units and selection rule differ].
f* is a floor: oracle token selection, recompute in isolation, no achievable scheme evaluated [0027]. The
rule, the tolerance and the band were registered before any prefill, and every figure in this paper
recomputes from raw dumps through a summarizer that refuses on disagreement [0023, 0028].

The claim, stated once. On 25 real SWE-bench handoffs whose sender prompts run 14K–30K tokens, the
same-model recompute floor is zero: not one matched token of any handoff exceeds τ_K [0029, FROZEN, cond. 1].
On 35 further handoffs of 35K–80K tokens under a receiver extended by YaRN to 81,920, it is zero again on
every handoff, after a bridge control shows τ_K carries to the scaled receiver [0036, FROZEN]. The residual is
local to the seam: pooled median deviation is 0.236 at the seam and 0.019 sixteen tokens out on the short
half, 0.260 and 0.063 on the long [0029, 0036]. A linear cross-model map through the same tokens sits past
DEGRADES on both halves, 0.9286 and 0.9640, and is reported as descriptive [0029, 0036]. Below the registered
tolerance the floor is not vacuous: at τ = 0.03 it reads 0.1433 on the short half and 0.5255 on the long
[0029, 0036], so length uses most of the headroom. Nothing in this paper is a downstream-quality number.

## 3. Draft related-work paragraph (≈ 0.25 page) — *proposed prose*

**Position-independent caching.** Prefix caching in vLLM and SGLang reuses KV only under an exact prefix
match. CacheBlend reuses non-prefix chunks and recomputes a selected subset of tokens; EPIC formalises
position-independent caching with boundary recomputation; PromptCache reuses modular prompt segments through
position slots; KVLink, APE and MiniPIC vary the selection, encoding and engine integration; KV Packet,
COMB and SemPIC move the repair into training. All are evaluated on retrieval or template-built
compositions. KVShareArena, concurrent with this work, benchmarks this line across prompt contexts and model
checkpoints on retrieved chunks and agent reports; it finds that position correction alone suffices until a
query draws on several sources, that unrepaired caches can fall below no cache at all, and that an adapter
fit on one checkpoint's caches loses quality on another. We measure the quantity these methods repair, the
post-position-correction residual, on real trajectories rather than compositions, at 14K–80K tokens, and
report it as an oracle floor rather than as a method.

**Cross-model KV transfer.** C2C and LatentAlign train per-pair fusers or adapters; DroidSpeak shares KV
between architecturally identical variants; Heo et al. fit a closed-form per-head ridge map within a family
and evaluate it on classification benchmarks over generic calibration text, retaining 73–98% of standalone
accuracy on four of six pairs. Our cross arm is the k = 1 member of that class, fit on 50 generic sequences,
read on agent text, and reported as descriptive; its shortfall on agent text at rest is measured separately
[0020, 0031, 0034].

**Terminology, recorded.** "Re-render / renderer" appears in agent infrastructure only in what was found: a
programmable chat-template library whose docs advise extending the sampled stream rather than re-rendering
history; chat-template discussion threads where a turn rendered one way at generation and another as
history invalidates the prefix cache on every request; agent-framework issue trackers on prefix instability.
No PIC paper found uses it. "Re-encoding" in KVShareArena means paying recompute to repair a cache and must
not be used as a synonym for re-rendering.
