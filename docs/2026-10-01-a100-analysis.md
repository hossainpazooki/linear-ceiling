The common threshold is the original Qwen mapper reference, not a calibrated quality
limit for each new model. The same original handoff texts were used, with model-specific
 tokenization and fixed YaRN settings; model and length effects are not isolated.
Hardware replicas are not additional handoffs. Intervals resample trajectories, retaining
all handoffs from a sampled trajectory. The six-case Qwen3-1.7B implementation bridge
passed its pre-specified bounds; maximum relative mean-error gap was 1.23258721479e-05.

For the original 35 long handoffs, deleting all tokens fewer than 16 receiver positions
after a seam leaves every retained mean above 0.03 (median 0.101553). These means retain
the original normalizers. This is a diagnostic deletion, not a boundary-repair experiment.
