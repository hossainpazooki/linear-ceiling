## R5 — "the sample is unrepresentative" (0032-A)

- **Claim under attack:** the ladder's p90 at τ = 0.03, same-model K, 0.5823 (entry 0029).
- **Recomputed:** `fstar_ladder_per_handoff.same_K["0.03"]` over 25 handoffs: median 0.1433, p10 0.0115, p90 0.5823, equal
  to the summary. The p90 position (rank 22 of 25) is handoff `django__django-10999_traj#54` (20241016 suite). Ranks 23
  and 24: `astropy-14182#68` (0.6010) and `astropy-7606#88` (0.9305).
- **Refutation test, as the row states it:** "the handoffs carrying the p90 are absent from the 09-08 review's two, OR
  those two were not drawn from the registered keep seed." The keep subset re-derives exactly from the sorted included
  set under seed 9, n 8 (eight ids, equal to `report.json` and `summary.json`). The review's two handoffs
  (`django-11066#36`, `astropy-14182#68`) are both in that subset. The p90 handoff is in neither the keep subset nor the
  review's two.
- **Finding: the attack FAILS.** The second clause is false. The first is literally true but does not bear on
  representativeness: the 09-08 record is a cold re-score of two archived kept dumps (an integrity check), not a sample
  of the ladder, and the p90 handoff was never kept, so no re-score could have reached it. One of the two re-scored
  handoffs sits one rank above the p90 and brackets it.
- **Limits:** the review record names handoffs by trace stem; mapped by stem. Whether the 25 handoffs represent the
  corpus is not a question this row's inputs can answer.

## R12 — "prefix invariance and pipeline identity reduce to one code path" (0032-B)

- **Claim under attack:** the two controls are distinct checks (entry 0025).
- **Code read (`e9.py`, `run_controls`):** identity scores the S dump against itself (`score_pairs` with the same source);
  prefix invariance runs a SEPARATE prefill of S plus the first receiver token into a second dump, then scores that dump
  against S on the same pairs file. Two halt rules (any nonzero square; the maximum token delta against 1e-4), two score
  records, re-checked separately by `summarize_e9`.
- **Recomputed:** identity `max_abs_square` 0.0; prefix `max_token_delta` 0.0 against tolerance 1e-4, extra token 785,
  29,391 positions; the two per-token files are byte-identical (same-model K and V all zero, identical reference arrays).
- **Finding: the attack FAILS.** The scoring arithmetic is one path, which entry 0025 concedes and gives as the reason the
  prefix control exists; the inputs are not one path.
- **Limits, stated plainly:** the mirror holds no log line of the S+1 prefill and the transient dump was deleted by
  design, so the separate prefill rests on the code and on the distinct prefix record, not on a captured dump.
  Byte-identical per-token files are what a correct box produces and also what a skipped prefill would produce. The
  check that would separate the two is R11 (a prefill on another handoff, batch shape or kernel), a GPU row.
