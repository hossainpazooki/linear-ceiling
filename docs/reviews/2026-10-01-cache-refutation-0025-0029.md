## Lead A: threshold-ladder sensitivity

### Claim and registered rule

Entry 0029, `ledger/ledger.md:1787`, states:

> Not one included handoff has a single matched token whose centered deviation exceeds τ_K

Entry 0023, beginning at line 1232, instead defines `f*(tau)` by the mean error of the retained
matched tokens. Removing the largest errors first minimizes the number removed for that
criterion. The registered same-model key band is HOLDS when the handoff median `f* <= 0.15`.
The descriptive tolerance ladder is registered in 0025 and reported at line 1782 of 0029.

### Attack attempted and evidence

Recompute `delta_K` from archived per-token squared differences and per-head centered receiver
variance; compute `f*` at the original `tau_K = 0.3186442653116294`, 0.1, and 0.03. Independently
count tokens above the original tolerance. Any exceedance defeats the quoted universal-token
claim, even when the remaining-mean statistic and its registered band reproduce.

| Cohort | Handoffs | Key tokens above tau_K / matched tokens | Median f*(tau_K) | Median f*(0.1) | Median f*(0.03) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native short (E9) | 25 | 9,047 / 155,257 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.1433 |
| Scaled short (E9s), descriptive | 25 | 10,336 / 155,257 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.2930 |
| Scaled long (E9l), descriptive context for this review | 35 | 30,701 / 387,508 | 0.0000 | 0.0119 | 0.5255 |

Every observation has zero `f*(tau_K)`. The two short rows contain the same handoffs under
different receiver configurations; they are not 50 independent handoffs. The long-row median
at 0.1 is positive and must not be described as zero.

For `20241016_composio_swekit/django__django-10999_traj#54`, 1,118 of 3,953 matched tokens exceed
`tau_K`, while mean key deviation is 0.22074303525884365 and `f*(tau_K) = 0`.

### Finding and limits

- **SURVIVES:** the original same-model key arithmetic and registered HOLDS band reproduce on
  the 25 native-short handoffs. Tightening the descriptive tolerance to 0.03 raises the median
  removal fraction to 0.1433. This sensitivity changes no registered outcome.
- **REFUTED:** the claim that every matched token is within tolerance. Entry 0038 already reports
  tokenwise exceedances; this review supplies the explicit counterexample and checked counts.
- **UNEVALUABLE:** whether these tolerances preserve generated-answer quality. The threshold
  is a mapper-error reference. Alternative calibration splits were not refitted in this review.

The source review's blanket description of `f*` as an oracle lower bound is also too strong.
Selected tokens are excluded from its denominator. Under an ideal full-cache criterion that
counts repaired tokens as exact, the resulting mean is `(n-k)/n` times the retained-token mean,
so meeting that full-cache criterion can require fewer repairs than `f*` reports. Actual partial
prefill can also propagate errors through reused states. Therefore report `f*` as an **oracle
token-removal diagnostic**, not a general lower bound on practical repair cost. Historical
ledger wording remains unchanged; this is a correction to its current interpretation.

## Lead B: exactly-zero prefix invariance

### Claim, registered control, and attack

Entry 0025, `ledger/ledger.md:1483`, requires the receiver to prefill `S` and separately prefill
`S` followed by the first receiver token. The maximum centered token-mean K/V deviation over
the original prefix must be at most `1e-4`; otherwise the run halts.

The attack checks whether the prefix control accidentally compares the same dump with itself,
and whether the available evidence establishes rejection of a deliberately corrupted state.

### Evidence

At the reviewed revision, `src/linear_ceiling/e9.py:240`–270 keeps two controls distinct:

- The identity score uses `same_src` as both inputs (lines 242–248).
- The prefix control writes `S_plus1.npy`, invokes a separate prefill into `same_src_plus1`, and
  compares that dump with `same_src` (lines 253–264). Its measured maximum is compared with the
  configured tolerance and raises on excess (lines 265–270).

The checked native E9 report records prefix `max_token_delta = 0.0`, tolerance `0.0001`, and
extra token `785`. The compact mirror used for this review does not contain the prefix-control
score/tensor files, so this review does not independently recompute that archived zero.

### Finding and limits

- **SURVIVES, source inspection:** the prefix control uses separate forward passes; it is not
  the same-object identity score. Equality is expected because an earlier causal state cannot
  attend to the appended token.
- **UNEVALUABLE here:** sensitivity of the complete historical dump/score/halt pipeline to a
  deliberately planted cache error. The original review did not run that test, and this CPU
  archive audit does not supply it. Reading the `worst > tol` branch is not an execution test.

A passing unchanged-prefix control checks a pipeline invariant. It does not establish agreement
after prompt re-rendering. Later scrambled-cache retrieval experiments test different behavior
and cannot substitute for this historical control.
