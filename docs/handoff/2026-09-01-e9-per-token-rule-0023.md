## Locked decisions

- **Verdict statistic = median f*(τ_K), not pooled R²** (0023). Reason: pooled R² is dominated
  by high-variance tokens/dimensions and has no tail; the reuse literature asks which tokens
  and how many. R² stays as a bridge and decides nothing.
- **δ is centered per layer-head (share of unexplained variance), not own-norm** (0023, rider 2).
  Reason: own-norm reads 0.084 where 1 − R² is 0.308 and blows up on small-norm tokens; centered
  makes the token mean exactly 1 − R². Own-norm is a one-line diagnostic only.
- **τ = mean (= 1 − R²), never median** (0023; learnings entry). Reason: the mapper's own f*
  under a median τ is 6–7%; under the mean it is 0 by construction, preserving 0019's anchor.
- **τ per read-out, never mixed** (rider 3): τ_K verdict-bearing; τ_V for the alongside f* only.
- **DEGRADES 0.50 is the operator's judgment, not a citation** (rider 1). Reason: only the 0.15
  edge has a literature anchor (CacheBlend); no second citation is manufactured.
- **f* is an oracle LOWER BOUND, always stated with both reasons** (restored-exactly; no error
  propagation through reused KV). Every output that prints f* carries the words.
- **Instrument described as per-head A5, head- and layer-averaged**; the 0.6917-vs-0.6814
  discrepancy is on the record (0023) rather than discovered later.
- **Table row untouched; header line notes the supersession.** House rule: only the verdict
  cell changes; the chain does not cover the table but the convention does.
- **Nothing in 0023's rule section is revisited after the first score file exists.**
