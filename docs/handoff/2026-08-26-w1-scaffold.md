## 1. What this repository is

`linear-ceiling` is week 1 of a 12-week program: *The Linear Ceiling: Predicting Cross-Model KV
Cache Transfer Before Fitting the Mapper.* The falsifiable claim is that the achievable fidelity of
moving KV cache state between two models can be predicted **from weights, before any mapper is
fitted**. Three things in the repo serve that claim and nothing else:

- **the screen** — regularized CCA giving a read-out-conditioned predicted R² (`src/linear_ceiling/screen.py`);
- **the seal** — the protocol that makes "predicted before fitted" auditable rather than asserted
  (`src/linear_ceiling/seal.py`);
- **the ledger** — pre-registered hypotheses whose verdicts stay `unresolved` until the experiment
  that decides them runs (`ledger/ledger.md`).

The fitting/injection/evaluation harness is the pinned, read-only upstream `kv-transfer-replication`
at `f3594458f73d70a15f195c863d52ea6592f61578` (`UPSTREAM.md`). It is **invoked, never vendored**.

six pairs × three λ. The table above is recomputed, not restated.

### 2.3 What the median hides — read this before re-specifying anything

The per-layer Δ(l) distributions are **not** noise around zero. They are strongly right-skewed, and
the skew is structured and reproducible:

| pair | n layers | median | p90 | max | layers with Δ ≥ 0.05 (= `delta_separate`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen3-0.6b-to-1.7b | 28 | +0.0193 | +0.1066 | +0.1212 | 7 — layer 0 and layers 22–27 |
| qwen3-4b-to-1.7b | 28 | +0.0108 | +0.0935 | +0.1244 | 6 — layer 0 and layers 23–27 |
| qwen3-0.6b-to-4b | 36 | +0.0109 | +0.0571 | +0.0776 | 5 — layer 0 and layers 31–34 |
| qwen3-1.7b-to-0.6b | 28 | +0.0062 | +0.0604 | +0.0740 | 5 — layer 0 and layers 24–27 |
| qwen3-4b-to-0.6b | 28 | +0.0074 | +0.0689 | +0.0828 | 5 — layer 0 and layers 24–27 |
| qwen3-1.7b-to-4b | 36 | +0.0043 | +0.0460 | +0.0682 | 4 — layer 0 and layers 32–34 |

(λ = 1e-3; the pattern is identical at 1e-2 and 1e-1 — the same layer indices, every time.)

**In every pair, at every λ, the layers exceeding `delta_separate` are the first block and the last
four to six blocks.** The middle of the network shows essentially no K–V separation and is what
drags the median under `delta_same`. The tail reaches +0.124 — two and a half times the SEPARATE
threshold.

This is a depth-dependent effect, and entry 0003 pre-registered the reason it might not be real:
"layer-0 embedding stands in for every layer's residual stream, so **depth-dependent nulls are the
proxy's**." The vocabulary-paired screen uses layer-0 embeddings as X and Y for every target layer,
so a systematic drift with depth is exactly what that stated limit predicts as an artifact. Two
readings survive the data:

- **real** — K and V genuinely occupy different canonical coordinates at the ends of the network
  and not in the middle; or
- **proxy artifact** — the layer-0 stand-in degrades with distance from the embedding, and the
  "separation" at depth is the proxy failing, not the model separating.

**E0 cannot distinguish them, and nothing here should be written as though it can.** E1, which uses
real residual streams instead of the embedding proxy, is what decides it.

### 2.4 Consequence under the frozen rule

SAME → **G1 degrade → Variant 3**. The screen, as operationalized in E0, does not earn the claim
that it discriminates K from V. Per the design spec all three termination states are publishable,
so Variant 3 is a destination, not a dead end.

Two things this verdict does **not** license:

- It does **not** resolve H-S2. H-S2 has two clauses; E0 decides only the first, on a *vocabulary
  proxy*, and the second clause (reproducing the measured K>V gap in predicted-R² ordering) belongs
  to E1. Whatever verdict entry 0004 sets for H-S2 must say which clause it is about.
- It does **not** mean K and V are interchangeable. The sign was consistent and the tails are large;
  what failed the test was *median magnitude under this proxy*.

## 6. Defects found in the plan itself, and how each was resolved

The build's reviewers found more wrong with the **plan** than with the implementations. Each is
recorded because the fix's reasoning is what future changes must not undo.

1. **Seal immutability bypass — CRITICAL, reproduced.** `_assert_committed_and_unmodified` used
   `git log --diff-filter=M`. Version control assigns no `M` status to a delete-then-re-add (`D`
   then `A`) or to a rename-away-then-rename-back (`R` at each end), so a **two-command sequence
   made a tampered seal pass both `require_sealed` and `verify_all`**. A reviewer reproduced this in
   a throwaway repo. **Resolved:** the rule is now "the sealed path must be touched by exactly
   **one** commit, ever" — plain `git log -- <path>` with no `--follow`, counting every status
   letter; the message names the offending commits. Proved by reverting to the old logic and
   confirming both new tests *fail to raise* against it. Verified present at
   `src/linear_ceiling/seal.py:107-125`.

2. **Seal writer unusable in a fresh clone.** The local artifact roots `mappers/` and
   `results/mapper/` did not exist, and `find_mapper_artifacts` fails closed on the first missing
   root — so **every** `write_prediction` refused with "root does not exist" before it could detect
   a real conflict, making fail-closed meaningless. **Resolved:** both roots ship as tracked
   `.gitkeep` placeholders with `.gitignore` negations (`git check-ignore` confirmed placeholders
   are addable while real artifacts stay ignored), so a missing root once again means the tree is
   genuinely unmounted.

3. **Ledger lint narrowed from 6 hypotheses to 2.** The plan brief contradicted itself
   (`REQUIRED_IDS` naming six ids, against a GOOD fixture registering only two). The implementer
   resolved it by **narrowing the lint to two ids** — the wrong direction, gutting the check the
   pre-registration rests on. **Resolved:** all six restored (verified at
   `src/linear_ceiling/ledger_check.py:8`), the fixture fixed instead of the lint, regression guard
   added.

4. **False provenance in a ledger entry.** Entry 0005's commentary cited "design-spec Appendix C" —
   but the design spec ends at Appendix B; Appendix C is in the **seed** doc. It also claimed
   `docs/gap-map.md` held the registered 0005 block, which it does not. Separately, 0005's bullets
   said "define the threshold in this entry" while its closing paragraph said thresholds are
   deliberately **not** set there. **Resolved:** provenance corrected to seed-w1 Appendix C and the
   tension recorded as a divergence — **with the registered block untouched**, since registered
   text is immutable. Only the repo's own commentary and a table header changed.

5. **Paraphrase-lint hole plus a crash on the reporting path.** Adding U+2192 (`→`) to the scope
   lint's sentence-boundary class opened an evasion: an arrow-joined paraphrase slipped through
   where the `;` variant was caught. In the same module `print("SCOPE:", p)` raised
   `UnicodeEncodeError` under cp1252 — crashing **exactly when reporting a real violation**.
   **Resolved:** punctuation gating replaced with stopword-filtered word-overlap similarity at
   threshold 0.45 (evasion case 0.5385 → caught with both arrow and semicolon; the real ledger's
   H-S3 text 0.3077 → clean **by measurement, not exemption**; the exemption list is still exactly
   the three verbatim docs), and prints made ASCII-safe with a test that opens a real cp1252 stream,
   proves the raw print raises, then proves the safe one does not.

6. **Plan arithmetic correction.** A brief's prose promised "20 passed" while its own code block
   defined 19 tests. The implementer refused to fabricate a twentieth and reported the true count.

7. **A gate that went red the moment the experiment it guards was run.**
   `tests/test_imports.py::test_results_tree_is_empty_placeholder` asserted that `results/` in the
   **working tree** contained nothing but placeholders, while its own docstring stated the invariant
   as "no experiment RESULTS are ever *committed*". Since `.gitignore` (`results/*`) already
   enforces the committed-side invariant, the test conflated *not committed* with *does not exist
   locally* — and failed the instant E0 wrote `results/e0/`, which is the designed behaviour. It was
   also **invisible in CI**, where a fresh clone has no `results/e0`, so the gate stayed green
   exactly for whoever had not run the experiment. **Resolved:** the check now asks
   `git ls-files -- results` — the index, not the filesystem — against an allowlist of
   `results/.gitkeep` and `results/mapper/.gitkeep`, with a fallback for git being unavailable or
   history being empty. Suite 107 → 108.

Two reviewers additionally corrected **claims of independence**: an implementer's "independent"
recomputation of the CCA identity reused the committed test's seeds and tolerance (the genuinely
independent check is the reviewer's closed-form derivation, which also byte-checked `r2_pooled`
against the upstream at the pin); and a pre-flight probe reported a checkpoint "not in cache"

Its conclusion survived re-checking, but **that probe is unreliable for pre-flight use until it

## 8. Known residual limitations — stated plainly

- **An amend or force-push that rewrites a sealing commit is undetectable by any local check.** The
  seal's immutability rule counts commits touching a path; rewriting history rewrites that count.
  Closing this needs an external anchor (a push to a remote with protected history, or a timestamp
  authority). Structural, not an oversight.
- **A green CI seal check proves hash integrity and commit immutability only. It never re-proves
  the pre-fit ordering.** `verify_all` deliberately does not consult the artifact roots, because CI
  has no upstream checkout. Nobody should read "CI seal-verify green" as "the prediction was made
  before the mapper was fitted." That ordering is proved by the *writer's* refusal at write time, on
  a machine that can see the upstream — and by nothing in CI.
- **CI has never actually run.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` is untracked and there is no remote. Its
  `fetch-depth: 0` is load-bearing (the seal's immutability check reads full history; a shallow
  clone blinds it), but that is an unexercised claim until the first push.
- **E0's screen is a proxy, and it says so.** Layer-0 embeddings stand in for every layer's residual
  stream, a uniform token prior is used, and the read-out is pre-norm `W_K` (D4). All three were
  stated in entry 0003 *before* running and none was tuned after seeing results — but they bound
  what a G1 verdict means, and §2.3 shows the depth limitation is not hypothetical.
- **`weights.heads()` is covered only by a tautological test** — the per-head row layout is an
  unverified modelling convention, unavoidable without network access. It is **unused** on the op-C
  path, which uses whole `k_proj`/`v_proj` as read-outs and never splits by head. It becomes
  load-bearing if a later experiment splits heads.
